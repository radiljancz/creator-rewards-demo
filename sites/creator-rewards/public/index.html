Before you explore.

Who is this designed for?

E-commerce teams with an existing creator partnership who want to test a more personal reward. The proposed starting point is one store, one creator and a clearly defined reward budget.

Is this ready to install in any store?

The experience has been demonstrated on a dedicated Shopify development store. Self-service installation and paid-order attribution are not yet established. This page links to the standalone demo and merchant studio.

Are the product designs real?

The featured Yosemite preview is a saved Arcade-generated result. It is a product preview, not evidence of a manufactured or purchased item. Each design and its lettering need review before production.

What would a pilot measure?

The proposed primary measure is contribution per referred visitor, compared with your current affiliate offer. Agree on full reward costs and attribution before launch. No conversion uplift is claimed by the prototype.
