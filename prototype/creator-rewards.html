From partnership to product

  1. Merchant sets the offer. A campaign budget, an agreed creator commission and eligible purchases.
  2. Creator makes a collection. Arcade turns their visual idea into a supported product. No compulsory merchant branding.
  3. Creator shares their link. Fans discover the collection inside the merchant's shopping journey.
  4. Fan chooses a product. Buy it separately in the future, receive a contribution, or unlock full coverage with an eligible basket.
  5. The budget is allocated. Creator commission + platform fee + product subsidy. The merchant retains the required order contribution.
  6. After the demo. Verify availability, delivered costs, payment and fulfillment; then test with one real partnership.

Shop installation, attribution, checkout, manufacturing and payouts are future integrations, not actions performed by this prototype.
